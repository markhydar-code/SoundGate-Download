Features

Everything your Mac's sound settings are missing

SoundGate lives in your menu bar and quietly takes care of where your sound goes and how it sounds.

Sound on your monitor

Route audio to your display's speakers automatically when it's connected, and switch back when it isn't.

Quick presets and a 10-band EQ, or a parametric Expert EQ with up to 16 bands you drag right on the graph.

A hearing test for your EQ

A few minutes of beeps in each ear finds what you hear less well — and builds a personal EQ, with a before/after to prove it.

Spatial audio for headphones

Hear music from speakers in front of you instead of inside your head — and with AirPods, they stay put when you turn.

Device profiles & priorities

Every speaker and headphone keeps its own sound, and your favourite connected device is always chosen first.

Control Center & menu bar

Quick volume buttons in Control Center, and everything else one click away in the menu bar.

Hearing test

An EQ tuned to your ears

Everyone hears a little differently — and every pair of headphones sounds different too. SoundGate measures both at once.

  • Short beeps in each ear find the quietest sound you can hear at each pitch
  • A personal EQ gently lifts what you hear less well
  • Compare before and after on speech or music, at matched loudness
  • Results are saved and named for your headphones

Not a medical test — it tunes your sound, it doesn't diagnose.

FAQ

Questions

Is SoundGate free?

Yes.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 14.2 (Sonoma) or later. Control Center buttons need macOS 26 or later.

Does it work with my headphones?

Any headphones get spatial audio's speakers-in-front effect. Head tracking needs AirPods (3rd generation or later), AirPods Pro, AirPods Max or supported Beats. If your AirPods already use Apple's “Spatialize Stereo”, turn that off in Control Center so the sound isn't processed twice.

My monitor's volume keys don't do anything on a Mac. Will this fix it?

Usually, yes. Most monitors let SoundGate change their speaker volume directly over the display cable (DDC). If yours doesn't, SoundGate tells you so in the app.

Why does it need System Audio Recording?

That's the macOS permission that lets an app process other apps' sound — needed for per-app volume, the EQ and spatial audio. SoundGate only processes the sound live; nothing is recorded.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit SoundGate from its menu, then drag SoundGate from Applications to the Trash. To also remove its settings, run defaults delete com.markhydar.SoundGate in Terminal.
