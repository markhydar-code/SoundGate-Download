SoundGate gives you control over your Mac's sound: route audio to your monitor (with working volume keys), set the volume of each app, tune an EQ to your ears with a hearing test, and make headphones sound like speakers in front of you with AirPods head tracking.

- Requires macOS 14.2 or later on Apple silicon
- Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple
- Runs entirely on your Mac — no account, no analytics

This repository hosts the website and installer releases.
